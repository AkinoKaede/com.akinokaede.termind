# Termind brand artwork

`termind.svg` is the master brand mark. Three white windows, interrupted
gray outlines (#6C7A83), and a blue terminal chevron (#38B6F4) define its shape.
Preserve the transparent canvas and white window interiors. Do not bake a
platform icon mask into the artwork.

`../paywall/` contains the light and dark feature illustrations.
The website's `public/brand_mark.svg` uses a tighter viewBox around the brand
geometry; `public/favicon.svg` strengthens outlines for browser tab sizes.

## Website visual guidelines

Use white raised surfaces on #F7F9FA, #26343D primary text, gray borders,
#38B6F4 decorative accents, and #086A9B for readable blue text and focus outlines.
Headings use Iowan Old Style / Palatino with Songti SC for Chinese; body text
uses Avenir Next with PingFang SC. Layered window ornaments belong to the hero;
content cards prioritize readable product details.

Keep the hero app icon's white fill. Check English, Simplified Chinese, and
Traditional Chinese layouts, keyboard focus, reduced motion, and 320px screens.

These guidelines describe the supplied assets and do not grant additional
permission to use or redistribute Termind branding.
